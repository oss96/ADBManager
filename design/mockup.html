Targets, not tabs

One device list covers ADB and fastboot. The selection is the target for every action, anywhere in the app. The toolbar always says how many devices will be affected.

A device keeps its row

Rows are merged by serial. When a phone reboots into the bootloader, only its chip changes, from Online to Bootloader, and the row stays where it is.

Everything is a job

Actions never block the window. Each job reports progress per device, can be cancelled, and ends with an exact count such as "2 succeeded, 1 failed".

Device states

Each state is shown by a glyph, a label and a colour together. Colour is never the only signal. Every pair meets WCAG AA in both themes.
